Michel Sanner is Professor of Molecular Biology in the Department of Integrative Structural and
Computational Biology at Scripps Research. He received his Ph.D. in Computer Science from the
University of Haute Alsace, France, in 1992.

His laboratory develops computational methods for predicting molecular interactions, including
the widely used AutoDock4 and tools for docking small molecules, proteins and peptides. He also
has a long-standing interest in software engineering: the lab builds reusable software components
that can be assembled quickly into custom applications, such as PMV, AutoDockTools and the visual
programming environment Vision.
