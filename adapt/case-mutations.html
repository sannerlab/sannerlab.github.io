Mutating residues

Replace a residue by any standard amino acid, to dock against a variant or to remove a side chain that gets in the way.

Right-clicking a residue under protein (or under modified) opens a menu of the standard amino acids to mutate it to.

The mutation is made when the receptor is written: the new side chain is built on the residue's backbone with the best-scoring rotamer, as for a side chain to complete. It is recorded in the file (REMARK 850 AGFR mutation B:SER:96:_ -> ILE) and applied to symmetry copies of the chain as well.

For a modified residue, the menu starts with its parent residue (Base Res: …), which returns it to the standard amino acid.
